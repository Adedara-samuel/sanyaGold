# W O Oyebamiji CPA LLC · Concept B: The Practice Seal

**Client:** W O Oyebamiji CPA LLC
**Direction:** Practice seal + wordmark

## Concept

A full professional seal that tells the firm's story at a glance:

- The name **W O Oyebamiji CPA LLC** runs around the top arc.
- **Tax · Audit · Attestation** reads upright along the bottom, naming the firm's core services.
- Brass separators mark the balance between them.
- At the centre, the **WO** monogram sits over a fine rule and **CPA**, framed by a heavy outer ring and fine inner rings.

In oxblood and antique brass, it carries the authority of an established, licensed practice and works as an official stamp on reports, certificates and engagement letters.

## Typography

- **Name & monogram:** Cormorant Garamond Bold
- **Seal lettering:** Manrope Bold, set on true circles (bottom arc reads upright)
- **Descriptor:** Manrope SemiBold (`CPA LLC`)

## Usage guidelines

- **Clear space:** at least ⅕ of the seal's diameter on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** the seal lettering needs the seal at **72 px / 20 mm or larger**. For smaller uses, use the name lockup without the seal.
- **Print:** ideal for embossing, foil stamping and rubber stamps.
- **Don't** rotate the seal lettering, change the ring weights, or place the seal over busy imagery.
