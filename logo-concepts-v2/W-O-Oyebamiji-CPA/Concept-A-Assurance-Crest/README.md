# W O Oyebamiji CPA LLC · Concept A: The Assurance Crest

**Client:** W O Oyebamiji CPA LLC
**Direction:** Crest emblem + wordmark

## Concept

A classic heater shield, drawn with a strong outer keyline and a fine inner keyline, stands for **protection**: compliance kept in order, filings defended, and clients represented with authority.

Inside, the firm's initials form a crest monogram: a precise geometric **W in navy** beside a perfect **brass O**, drawn at one height and one stroke weight so they read as a single, balanced mark. **CPA** sits beneath on a fine brass rule. With the service line *Tax · Audit · Attestation*, the identity states exactly who the firm is and what it does, with no checkmarks, scales or columns.

## Typography

- **Name:** Playfair Display SemiBold, uppercase, tracking +100
- **Descriptor:** Manrope SemiBold (`CPA LLC`), widely tracked
- **Service line:** Manrope Medium (`TAX · AUDIT · ATTESTATION`)

## Usage guidelines

- **Clear space:** at least ¼ of the crest's height on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** crest 28 px / 8 mm tall; horizontal logo 180 px / 45 mm wide.
- **Backgrounds:** full colour on white or Mist; reversed version on Navy.
- **Don't** fill the shield solid, thicken the inner keyline, separate the W and O, or add effects.
