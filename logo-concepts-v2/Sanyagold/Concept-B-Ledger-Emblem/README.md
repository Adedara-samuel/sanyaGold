# Sanyagold · Concept B: The Ledger S

**Client:** Sanyagold Accounting, Advisory and Consulting Services
**Direction:** Ledger emblem + wordmark

## Concept

A circular emblem filled with fine, evenly spaced **ledger lines**: the organized records, reconciled accounts and dependable payroll at the core of Sanyagold's work. Running through them is a **bold gold S**, for Sanyagold and for the value and clarity the firm draws out of the numbers.

The lines part precisely around the S, showing order and insight working together, and a fine outer ring frames a complete, trusted service. It is modern, structured and confident, and it reads clearly from a business card to a building sign.

## Typography

- **Wordmark:** Jost Medium, uppercase, tracking +160
- **Descriptor & service line:** Jost Regular, uppercase, widely tracked
- **Documents:** Montserrat

## Usage guidelines

- **Clear space:** at least ¼ of the emblem's diameter on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** emblem 24 px / 7 mm; horizontal logo 180 px / 45 mm wide.
- **One colour:** the gaps around the S keep it legible in black or white.
- **Don't** change the number or spacing of ledger lines, close the gaps around the S, place the emblem over busy photography, or add effects.
