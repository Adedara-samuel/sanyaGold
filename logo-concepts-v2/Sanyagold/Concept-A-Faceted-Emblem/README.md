# Sanyagold · Concept A: The Six-Facet Gold

**Client:** Sanyagold Accounting, Advisory and Consulting Services
**Direction:** Faceted emblem + wordmark

## Concept

A hexagonal cut of gold, seen from above. Its **six crown facets** stand for the firm's six disciplines: accounting, book-keeping, payroll, HR, business advisory and consulting. All six meet at an **evergreen centre**, the client, at the heart of everything the firm does.

Shaded in three golds like a real cut stone, the emblem speaks of value, precision and care. Its hexagon carries forward the shape of Sanyagold's existing mark, so the brand evolves rather than starts over. The service line beneath the name says plainly what the firm does.

## Colour palette

| Colour | HEX | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evergreen | `#0E4A3B` | Centre facet (the client), wordmark, dark backgrounds |
| Light Gold | `#E2C98F` | Lit facets |

| Deep Gold | `#93723A` | Shadow facets, service line |
| Paper | `#F4F1EA` | Backgrounds |

## Typography

- **Wordmark:** EB Garamond Medium, uppercase, tracking +200
- **Descriptor & service line:** Montserrat Medium, uppercase, widely tracked
- **Documents:** EB Garamond Regular (headings), Montserrat (text)

## Usage guidelines

- **Clear space:** at least ¼ of the emblem's height on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** emblem 20 px / 6 mm; horizontal logo 180 px / 45 mm wide. The service line can be dropped below 220 px.
- **One colour:** the black and white versions keep every facet through the gaps.
- **Don't** recolour individual facets outside the palette, close the gaps between facets, rotate the hexagon, or add gradients or bevels; the dimension comes from the three flat golds.
