# Sanyagold · Concept B: The Hallmark

**Client:** Sanyagold Accounting, Advisory and Consulting Services
**Direction:** Premium typographic identity / lettermark

## Concept

A hallmark is the mark stamped on genuine gold to certify its quality, a fitting metaphor for a firm whose work is built on accuracy and trust. The **SG** lettermark sits inside a chamfered octagonal cartouche drawn with a strong outer line and a fine inner line, recalling a traditional assay punch.

The same octagon replaces the **O** in the wordmark and is set in champagne, carrying one distinctive, ownable detail into the name itself. Classical capitals with generous spacing, a fine rule and a restrained palette convey an established, considered professional practice.

## Typography

- **Wordmark:** Cormorant Garamond SemiBold, uppercase, tracking +200, with a custom octagonal O
- **Lettermark:** Cormorant Garamond Bold
- **Descriptor:** Montserrat Medium, uppercase, tracking +300

## Arrangements

- **Primary:** stacked (hallmark, wordmark, rule with diamond, descriptor)
- **Horizontal:** hallmark, divider, wordmark and descriptor
- **Lettermark only:** for favicons, seals, embossing and small spaces
- **Wordmark:** wordmark, rule and descriptor

## Usage guidelines

- **Clear space:** at least ¼ of the hallmark's height on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** lettermark 24 px / 7 mm tall; stacked logo 140 px / 35 mm wide. Below that, use the lettermark alone.
- **Backgrounds:** full colour on white or Warm Stone; reversed version on Charcoal.
- **Print:** the hallmark suits blind embossing, foil stamping (champagne/gold foil) and wax-seal style applications.
- **Don't** replace the octagonal O with a round O, stretch or rotate the logo, thicken the fine inner line, or add effects.
