# Sanyagold · Concept A: The Golden Line

**Client:** Sanyagold Accounting, Advisory and Consulting Services
**Direction:** Geometric symbol + wordmark

## Concept

A monoline **S** drawn on a strict square grid with one consistent stroke weight. Every proportion is fixed to the grid. The outer terminals are cut at a precise 45° and the form is point-symmetric, giving a sense of order, accuracy and balance. Measured gaps separate the strokes, like the clean break between accounts that still reconcile.

Through the centre runs **the golden line**, set in champagne gold: the connecting thread across bookkeeping, payroll, HR and advisory. Paired with spaced Garamond capitals in deep evergreen, the identity feels established, calm and quietly confident. It avoids every accounting cliché (no books, calculators, currency, charts or buildings).

## Typography

- **Wordmark:** EB Garamond Medium, uppercase, tracking +200
- **Descriptor:** Montserrat Medium, uppercase, tracking +280
- **Documents & stationery:** EB Garamond Regular (headings), Montserrat (supporting text)

## Arrangements

- **Primary:** horizontal (symbol, gold divider, wordmark and descriptor)
- **Stacked:** for square or centred layouts
- **Symbol only:** for favicons, social avatars, stamps and small spaces
- **Wordmark:** where the symbol already appears nearby

## Usage guidelines

- **Clear space:** keep a margin of at least ¼ of the symbol's height on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** symbol 16 px / 5 mm tall; horizontal logo 160 px / 40 mm wide.
- **Backgrounds:** use full colour on white or Paper White; use the reversed or white version on Evergreen or photography.
- **One colour:** use the black or white versions when colour isn't available. The gaps keep the S legible.
- **Don't** stretch, rotate, recolour the strokes individually, add effects or shadows, close the gaps, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
