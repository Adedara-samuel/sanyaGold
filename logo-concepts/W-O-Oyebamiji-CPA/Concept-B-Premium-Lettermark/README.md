# W O Oyebamiji CPA LLC · Concept B: The Seal

**Client:** W O Oyebamiji CPA LLC
**Direction:** Premium seal / lettermark

## Concept

For a licensed CPA firm, a seal carries exactly the right message: an official mark of review, verification and professional responsibility. The **WO** monogram, in a high-contrast classical serif, sits at the centre of a precisely drawn double ring, with the firm's full name set around it on a true circle.

Oxblood and antique brass give the identity the weight of an established practice, while generous spacing and fine rules keep it refined rather than ornamental. The seal also works on its own as a stamp of approval on reports, engagement letters and certificates.

## Typography

- **Name:** Playfair Display SemiBold, uppercase, tracking +100 (`W O OYEBAMIJI`)
- **Monogram:** Playfair Display Bold (`WO`)
- **Descriptor & seal lettering:** Manrope SemiBold, uppercase, widely tracked

## Arrangements

- **Primary:** stacked (seal, name, rule with diamond, `CPA LLC`)
- **Horizontal:** seal, divider, name and descriptor
- **Seal only:** for stamps, certificates, embossing and social avatars
- **Wordmark:** name and descriptor only

## Usage guidelines

- **Clear space:** at least ⅕ of the seal's diameter on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** the seal's ring lettering needs space. Use the seal at 72 px / 20 mm or larger. For smaller uses (e.g. favicons) use the wordmark, or ask for a simplified seal without ring lettering.
- **Backgrounds:** full colour on white or Parchment; reversed version on Oxblood.
- **Print:** well suited to embossing, foil stamping and rubber-stamp applications.
- **Don't** rotate the ring lettering, change the ring weights, place the seal on busy imagery, or add effects.
