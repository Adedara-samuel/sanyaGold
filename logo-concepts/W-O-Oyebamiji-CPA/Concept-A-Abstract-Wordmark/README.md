# W O Oyebamiji CPA LLC · Concept A: The Interlock

**Client:** W O Oyebamiji CPA LLC
**Direction:** Geometric symbol + wordmark

## Concept

Two monoline squares drawn on one strict grid with a single stroke weight: one in **navy** for the client's records, one in **brass** for independent review. Offset and woven together, the navy square passes over the brass square at the first crossing and under it at the second, with a precise gap at each crossing.

Neither square can be pulled away without the other. That is the essence of assurance: your records and an independent, professional review locked together into one trustworthy whole. Exact geometry and a clean sans-serif wordmark keep the identity modern, rigorous and highly legible, with no checkmarks, scales, columns or other CPA clichés.

## Typography

- **Name:** Manrope Bold, uppercase, tracking +120 (`W O OYEBAMIJI`)
- **Descriptor:** Manrope SemiBold, uppercase, tracking +600 (`CPA LLC`)
- **Documents & stationery:** Montserrat

## Arrangements

- **Primary:** horizontal (symbol, brass divider, name and descriptor)
- **Stacked:** for square or centred layouts
- **Symbol only:** for favicons, social avatars and small spaces
- **Wordmark:** name and descriptor only

## Usage guidelines

- **Clear space:** at least ¼ of the symbol's height on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** symbol 20 px / 6 mm tall; horizontal logo 160 px / 40 mm wide.
- **Backgrounds:** full colour on white or Mist; reversed version on Navy.
- **One colour:** the black and white versions keep the over-under weave through the gaps.
- **Don't** close the crossing gaps, swap which square passes over, change the square proportions, or add effects.
